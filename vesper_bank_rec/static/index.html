01 · SOURCES & OPENING

Make the starting point trustworthy.

Use a bank statement independent from the accounting ledger. Both sources need their own opening, signed activity and closing controls. Refreshing a source resets match decisions.

Import a bank statement or book ledger CSV
CSV column mapping

Each CSV needs date, description, signed amount, exact account ID and currency. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD; inflows are positive, outflows negative. Blank transaction rows are rejected. Duplicate occurrences are preserved.

Review the opening reconciliation anchor

Each item needs date, description, amount and evidence. Example: [{"date":"2026-08-28","description":"Check 1001","amount":"-100.00","evidence":"Prior reconciliation check schedule"}]. The itemized amounts must explain the opening bank/book difference exactly.

02 · MATCH REVIEW

Suggestions are a starting point.

Signed amounts must agree exactly. Date and memo similarity rank one-to-one candidates. Review each pair before confirming. Split or batch matching requires external resolution and a refreshed ledger.

Connect on your terms.

The app runs locally. Live model calls and QuickBooks reads contact their providers. You pay your provider’s API charges. Credentials use your OS credential store when available; session-only storage is available without plaintext persistence.

2. Accounting reads · QuickBooks Online

Use your own Intuit developer app. Start with a sandbox company. Register exactly https://developer.intuit.com/v2/OAuth2Playground/RedirectUrl, authorize Accounting access in Intuit’s official OAuth Playground ↗, and enter the returned Realm ID and refresh token here. Production additionally requires eligible production credentials and an authorized live-company grant.

No Vesper secret or hosted callback is included. This connector reads posted ledger records, not pending bank-feed transactions. Your independent bank statement is still required.
