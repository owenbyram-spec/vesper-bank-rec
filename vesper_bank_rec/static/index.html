01 · SOURCES & OPENING

Make the starting point trustworthy.

Use a bank statement independent from the accounting ledger. Both sources need their own opening, signed activity and closing controls. Refreshing a source resets match decisions.

Import a bank statement or book ledger CSV
CSV column mapping

Each CSV needs date, description, signed amount, exact account ID and currency. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD; inflows are positive, outflows negative. Blank transaction rows are rejected. Duplicate occurrences are preserved.

Review the opening reconciliation anchor

Each item needs date, description, amount and evidence. Example: [{"date":"2026-08-28","description":"Check 1001","amount":"-100.00","evidence":"Prior reconciliation check schedule"}]. The itemized amounts must explain the opening bank/book difference exactly.

02 · MATCH REVIEW

Suggestions are a starting point.

Signed amounts must agree exactly. Date and memo similarity rank one-to-one candidates. Review each pair before confirming, and record the evidence and who performed the review. Names are self-reported. Split or batch matching requires external resolution and a refreshed ledger.

03 · EXCEPTIONS

Give every remaining item a reason.

Outstanding deposits and withdrawals carry forward with their original identities. Bank fees, interest and returned payments stay pending until posted externally and matched to a refreshed ledger.
